# Local performance baseline — 2026-10-12

Executed the dependency-free measurement runner with 1,000 warmup operations
and 2,000 measured operations per client, two independent JVM forks per case,
one/four caller threads, and a fixed 256 MiB heap. All workload-output checks
passed. Temurin 25.0.4.1 was used; complete machine/JDK details are in
[environment metadata](2026-10-12-environment.txt), with all observations in
[raw CSV](2026-10-12-results.csv).

The ranges below show the two observed forks; they are not confidence intervals.
Latency includes each library call. Throughput also includes pool coordination
and measurement bookkeeping. Clock reads affect small direct-call measurements.
These short runs are diagnostic and do not establish warmed steady-state
performance or production service-level guarantees.

The direct and sequential cases execute the same 16 operations. Other cases
have different work and should not be ranked as interchangeable alternatives.
Parallel branches are CPU-only and intentionally small, so their scheduling
cost does not predict the benefit of parallel network calls. The capacity
case performs one operation with four available slots; four clients alone do
not establish behavior under heavy contention.

Heap usage delta is an occupancy observation, not allocation per operation.
JMH with its GC profiler is configured but has not run locally because its
plugins/dependencies were unavailable and DNS prevented downloading them.
Use [benchmark instructions](../../java/benchmarks/README.md) to collect longer
JMH runs before optimizing or establishing regression thresholds.
